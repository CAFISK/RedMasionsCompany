## 三、使用说明

### 完整 Prompt 组合方式（角色立绘）

生成角色立绘时，将 **角色基础 Prompt** + **表情描述追加 Prompt** 组合使用。

**示例** — 生成贾宝玉的"开心"表情：
```
ancient Chinese anime style, gufeng, delicate lineart, soft cel-shading, male character, visual novel character sprite, transparent background, knee-up portrait, left-top soft lighting. A handsome young man around 30 with a boyish charm and slightly lazy demeanor. He wears a loose white dress shirt with the collar slightly open, layered with a modernized dark crimson Chinese vest (马甲) with cloud-pattern embroidery on the cuffs. Long black hair tied in a low ponytail with a few loose strands framing his forehead. A jade pendant necklace (modernized Tongling Baoyu). Gentle, expressive dark eyes. Slim build. Bright cheerful smile, sparkling eyes, slightly tilted head, one hand raised in a small wave. High quality, detailed
```

### 生成参数建议

| 参数 | 背景图 | 角色立绘 |
|------|--------|----------|
| 尺寸 | 1280×720 (16:9) | 768×1024 或 832×1216 |
| 采样步数 | 28-35 | 28-35 |
| CFG Scale | 7-8 | 7-8 |

### 风格一致性注意事项
